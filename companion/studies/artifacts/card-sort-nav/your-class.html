Knowing and loving your class

  • I'm new to the calling (or the class is new to me) and I don't know my students or their needs.
  • A student is hard to love — disruptive or draining — and I feel frustrated with them.
  • Students are afraid to participate for fear of being wrong; the class doesn't feel safe.
  • Students don't feel needed; attendance and investment are slipping.
  • I want to really understand what a student means, or where a comment is coming from.
