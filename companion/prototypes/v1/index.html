The first functional prototype — two example turn-ins

Same assignment, same rubric, and each team building on its own tree test: color and layout now doing real work, and a search feature invented from study vocabulary rather than a study of its own. Both are real, working artifacts — browse, search, try the log exports, and form your own opinion before reading any grading.

Team A

Their tree test's six decisions built exactly as written — including the trial branch its next study exists to judge. One data file, three views (two hierarchies and search), a hand-built concept layer behind the search box, facet chips to narrow results, and a click-and-query log you can export.

Alternative build — semantic search

An instructor exploration, not a turn-in: Team A’s prototype with one thing changed — the search matches what you mean, not just the words you type. “Kids are noisy,” “teenagers on their phones,” “was that the Spirit” all land, from a meaning index computed once ahead of time. Still no server, and no AI call at lookup time. Compare it against the original and read its “About this search” page.
