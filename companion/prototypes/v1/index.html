The prototype, evolving — the instructor’s revision line

A prototype in the graded chain freezes when its study runs; the thinking doesn’t. These builds revise Team A’s prototype one bet at a time, each loading the previous build’s work by reference rather than copying it. Read them in order and watch what each revision changes, what it refuses to touch, and what it admits it can’t know without the next study.

v2Semantic search

One subsystem replaced: the search matches what you mean, not just the words you type. “Kids are noisy,” “teenagers on their phones,” “was that the Spirit” all land, from a meaning index computed once ahead of time. Still no server, and no AI call at lookup time.

v3Skill cards

The newest revision: every listing redesigned from a title row into an informative card — the situation you’re in (teacher words, on all 53 blocks now), the manual’s exact sentence, the first move to try, and chips for lesson moment and how much preparing it takes — plus a simpler, whiter shell and a “narrow by preparation” search facet. The data, hierarchy, and meaning index are v2’s, untouched.
