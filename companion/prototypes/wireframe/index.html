The wireframe assignment — two example turn-ins

Two teams, same assignment, same card-sort method — very different wireframes. Both are real, working artifacts: click through them, try the log exports, and form your own opinion before reading any grading.

The wireframe is Turn-in 2, handed in and graded on its own on the 60-point Prototype Rubric. The tree test each team then ran on its own wireframe is Turn-in 3, a separate report on the Study Report Rubric. Both wireframe reports, graded → · Both tree tests, graded →
